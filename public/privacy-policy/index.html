1. Introduction (পরিচিতি)

SAJGOJ FASHION ("we", "our", "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal data you share when visiting https://sajgoj.nfbd.workers.dev, using our mobile application, or interacting with our Facebook / WhatsApp integrations.

2. Information We Collect (আমরা যে তথ্য সংগ্রহ করি)

  • Contact & Delivery Details: Full name, mobile phone number, district/thana, delivery address, and optional email address when placing an order or creating an account.
  • Social / Account Authentication Data: If you sign in with Google or Facebook Login (via Meta Developer App / Firebase Auth), we receive only your basic profile name, email address, and profile avatar solely for account authentication and order history synchronization.
  • Order & Transaction Metadata: Ordered items, SKU, invoice number, payment method preference (Cash on Delivery, bKash, Nagad, Rocket, Card), and courier tracking status. We never store raw credit/debit card PINs or mobile banking PINs.
  • Device & Technical Information: Essential local storage / session data, PWA offline cache, and basic analytics used strictly to keep your shopping cart intact and improve site speed.

3. How We Use Your Information (তথ্য ব্যবহারের উদ্দেশ্য)

  • To process, confirm, pack, and deliver your orders accurately via courier partners (Steadfast, Pathao, RedX).
  • To send order confirmation, invoice, shipping tracking, and customer support updates via SMS, WhatsApp, email, or push notifications.
  • To prevent fraudulent orders, verify cash-on-delivery requests, and provide 7-day return/exchange service.

4. Data Sharing & Third-Party Services (তৃতীয় পক্ষের সাথে তথ্য শেয়ার)

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third-party advertisers. Information is shared strictly on a need-to-know basis with:

  • Cloud Infrastructure: Google Firebase (Authentication & Cloud Firestore) for secure encrypted cloud data storage.
  • Courier & Delivery Partners: Name, phone, and shipping address only for physical parcel delivery.
  • Meta / Facebook Services: Only when you explicitly use Facebook Login, Messenger support, or Catalog features subject to Meta Platform Terms.

5. Data Retention, Security & Your Rights (তথ্য সুরক্ষা ও আপনার অধিকার)

You have full rights to access, update, export, or request permanent deletion of your personal information at any time. To request account or Facebook App data removal, please visit our dedicated User Data Deletion page (https://sajgoj.nfbd.workers.dev/data-deletion) or contact us directly.

6. Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for general audiences and users legally able to place online orders (13+ with guardian consent). We do not knowingly collect sensitive personal data from children under 13.
